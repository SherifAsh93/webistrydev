# Personalized outreach and inquiry handling

Replace bracketed sections with a genuine observation. Send individually to relevant
businesses; stop after a decline. Do not send the same bulk pitch to unrelated Pages.

## First message — Arabic

أهلاً [الاسم]، أنا شريف من WebistryDev. شفت [منتج/خدمة محددة] عندكم، وعجبني
[تفصيل حقيقي]. لاحظت إن [خطوة فعلية في رحلة الطلب/التصفح ممكن تبقى أوضح].

بشتغل على مواقع ومتاجر، وعندي مثال قريب من نشاطكم. تحب أبعتلك مثال قصير
ونشوف لو الفكرة مناسبة لطريقة شغلكم؟

## First message — English

Hi [name], I’m Sherif from WebistryDev. I came across [specific product/service]
and liked [genuine detail]. I noticed [one factual opportunity in the browsing,
booking, or inquiry flow].

I build websites and online stores, and have an example relevant to your business.
Would you like me to send a brief example so you can see whether it fits?

## After they agree to see an example

Share one relevant public project link, explain one feature, and ask:

- What would you most like customers to be able to do online?
- Are you exploring the idea, or do you have a preferred launch timeframe?

Do not overwhelm the prospect with the entire portfolio or promise a sales increase.

## One follow-up — 3–5 working days later

أهلاً [الاسم]، متابعة بسيطة بخصوص [الفكرة]. لو تحسين [الخطوة المحددة] ضمن
خططكم، يسعدني نحدد المطلوب في مكالمة قصيرة. ولو التوقيت مش مناسب، مفيش مشكلة.

Hi [name], a quick follow-up on [specific idea]. If improving [the workflow] is
on your roadmap, I’d be happy to discuss a practical scope. If the timing isn’t
right, no problem.

## Handling incoming inquiries

1. Acknowledge the actual business idea, rather than sending a generic price list.
2. Ask for the main customer action, required pages/features, and target timeframe.
3. Ask about budget expectations after understanding the goal.
4. Suggest a suitable scope and explain what is excluded.
5. Provide a written quote, timeline, review stages, and payment terms.
6. Agree the next action and its date; log it in the lead dashboard.
