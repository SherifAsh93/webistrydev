# WebistryDev — fresh 30-day client-acquisition launch

## Direction

Target business owners in Egypt and abroad. Lead with retail brands, furniture
showrooms, and service businesses because the portfolio can demonstrate those
workflows directly. Use Arabic and English posts separately, each with one offer
and one action. The working source stays on the VPS; approved website releases
go to Vercel.

**Core offer:** a free 15-minute project discussion, followed by a proposed scope,
timeline, and quote. The discussion is not a promise of free design/development.

**Positioning:** "A clear online home for your products, services, and customer
requests — built around your business, with direct access to the developer."

**Primary CTA:** tell Sherif your business type on WhatsApp or request a project
discussion. Use one of those actions per post, rather than several competing CTAs.

## New measurement baseline

For every inquiry, record source/post, business type, requested outcome, discussion
date, quoted scope, and next step. Count qualified conversations, quotes, and paid
projects separately. A WhatsApp click alone is not a confirmed inquiry.

Campaign links in `POSTS.md` include UTM tags. Site Contact/Lead hooks are ready
for the existing Meta Pixel integration when a WebistryDev Pixel is connected.
Start counting from launch day; no previous screenshots or historical exports are
needed to run this plan.
