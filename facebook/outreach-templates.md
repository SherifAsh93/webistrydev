# 🎯 Direct Outreach Templates — Get Your First Client

## How to Use These Templates

1. Find 20 businesses with bad/no website on Google Maps or Facebook
2. Send the appropriate template via WhatsApp or Facebook Messenger
3. Follow up after 2-3 days if no response
4. Create a free mockup for interested businesses

شوفت إن العيادة محتاجة موقع يشتغل على الموبايل وفيه حجز اونلاين — وده بالضبط اللي بعمله.

بنيت موقع لعيادة البطراوي فيه:
✅ حجز مواعيد اونلاين

## 🎯 Daily Action Plan

**Day 1-3:** Find 20 businesses with bad/no website
**Day 4-7:** Send 5 messages per day
**Day 8-10:** Follow up with non-responders
**Day 11-14:** Create free mockups for interested businesses
**Day 15+:** Close the deal!

---

## 💡 Tips for Success

1. **Personalize every message** — Use the business name and specific details
2. **Show, don't tell** — Create a free mockup before asking for money
3. **Follow up** — Most people don't respond to the first message
4. **Be specific** — Mention a specific problem you noticed
5. **Offer value first** — Free mockup, free consultation, free audit

---

## 🚫 What NOT to Do

1. Don't send generic mass messages
2. Don't ask for money before showing value
3. Don't give up after 1-2 no responses
4. Don't compete on price — compete on quality and service
5. Don't forget to follow up!
