# WebistryDev Manifesto

## Our Mission

WebistryDev exists to build modern, reliable, and scalable software that solves
real business problems.

We believe software should be engineered, not merely assembled.

Every project should improve not only the client's business, but also strengthen
WebistryDev itself through reusable knowledge, better standards, and continuous
engineering improvements.

---

## Our Vision

To build a software engineering company whose greatest asset is its knowledge
rather than any specific technology, framework, or AI model.

Technology changes.

Engineering principles remain.

WebistryDev is designed to evolve continuously while keeping ownership of its
architecture, standards, and engineering process.

---

## Our Philosophy

We do not chase trends.

We adopt technology only when it improves quality, maintainability, scalability,
or developer productivity.

AI is an engineering tool.

It assists.

It accelerates.

It never replaces engineering judgment.

Every important technical decision belongs to the lead engineer.

Knowledge belongs to WebistryDev.

---

# Engineering Principles

## 1. Knowledge is the Company's Greatest Asset

Every completed project must improve WebistryDev.

Code, documentation, reusable components, engineering decisions, lessons
learned, and workflows must be preserved so future projects become faster and
better.

---

## 2. The Lead Engineer Makes Technical Decisions

AI assists with implementation.

AI may suggest improvements.

AI never changes architecture, technologies, libraries, or business logic
without explicit approval.

---

## 3. Build Once, Reuse Forever

Whenever something can be reused in another project, it should be extracted into
reusable knowledge, reusable code, or a reusable blueprint.

---

## 4. Simplicity Over Complexity

The simplest maintainable solution is preferred over the most complicated one.

Avoid unnecessary technologies, dependencies, and abstractions.

---

## 5. Continuous Improvement

## Every finished project leaves WebistryDev stronger than before.

# What We Believe

We believe software is an engineering discipline.

Every architecture decision should be intentional.

Every project should become an asset for future projects.

---

We believe AI is one of the greatest engineering tools ever created.

AI does not replace engineering.

---

Knowledge should belong to WebistryDev.

- Scalable

- Built with care

Not just software that works today.
